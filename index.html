Guidelines & Rulebooks

1

Fractions Configuration Options

Rational Fractions can be set to Similar, Dissimilar, or Mixed Numbers. Teachers or students can customize numerator and denominator digit lengths (1–4 digits) and clamp the denominator range from 1 to 100.

2

Entering Answers in Practice Mode

Learners can input reduced improper fractions (e.g. -7/3), mixed numbers with a space (e.g. -2 1/3), or integers (e.g. 4). The mathematical engine checks exact fractional equivalence automatically.
