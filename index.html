How to Use PROJECT PRIME

  • Select any of the 7 mathematics modules from the Home dashboard.
  • For Whole Numbers, choose whether to practice standard digits (1–10 digits) or focus on specific number fields / multiplication tables from 1 to 12.
  • Customize parameters: 5–100 questions, regrouping modes, and multi-term operations.
  • Type answers directly in the visual math box. Use xⁿ to type raised superscripts without needing any ^ caret characters.
  • Take advantage of 3 attempts per problem with progressive hints and sample solutions.
  • Enter your name at the end to create your printable certificate and record your score.
